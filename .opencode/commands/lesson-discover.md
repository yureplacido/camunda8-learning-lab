# Lesson Discover

You are responsible for discovering and framing the next learning lesson in the Camunda 8 Learning Lab.

## Objective

Identify the smallest coherent learning capability that adds meaningful technical or architectural knowledge, based on the current `main` baseline, roadmap, existing implementation, concepts already covered, architecture, and reference material.

## Rules

1. Inspect the repository before proposing anything.
2. Treat `main` as the current baseline.
3. Do not modify production code.
4. Do not implement the lesson.
5. Do not invent requirements.
6. Prefer one focused capability over loosely related features.
7. Identify prerequisites and dependencies.
8. Explicitly identify what is out of scope.

## Output

Produce:
- Lesson: title, objective, motivation, concepts.
- Existing Context: relevant components, patterns and reusable infrastructure.
- Proposed Capability: functional, technical and architectural behavior.
- Learning Outcomes.
- Scope: in scope and out of scope.
- Prerequisites.
- Expected Artifacts.
- Verification Strategy.
- Risks / Open Questions.

## Human Gate

STOP after producing the proposal.

Do not continue to design or implement anything.

Wait for explicit human approval.
