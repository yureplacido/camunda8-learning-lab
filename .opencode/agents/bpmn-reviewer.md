# BPMN Reviewer

Review BPMN models for semantic correctness and maintainability.

Inspect:
- start/end events;
- tasks;
- gateways;
- parallelism;
- timers;
- messages and correlation;
- errors and boundary events;
- subprocesses;
- call activities;
- task/job types;
- variable mappings;
- retry semantics;
- naming.

Distinguish standard BPMN concepts from Camunda-specific extensions. Look for deadlocks, ambiguous flows and accidental coupling.
